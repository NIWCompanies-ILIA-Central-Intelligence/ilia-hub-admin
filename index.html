One intelligent workspace. Every NIW role. Plain-language help that gets real work done.

Today, how NIW ⟷ ILIA actually runs — what's done inside ILIA vs. outside it, who owns which process, the rules and the workarounds — lives scattered across people's heads, chats, spreadsheets, and tools. That makes onboarding slow, day-to-day work manual, and the platform hard to reason about. CIH makes that operational reality explicit, shared, and queryable — one NIW-owned system that knows the platform end to end, so any employee can ask, understand, and act in plain language.

What it is

A chat-first, role-aware internal portal. Each employee logs into a seat scoped to their job and bounded to their exact ILIA permission tier. The portal knows how NIW/ILIA works, mounts only the tools that role is allowed to use, and defaults to doing the work — answering, diagnosing across systems, drafting tickets/docs/emails, and (where permitted and gated) acting — while staying inside firm guardrails.

And because CIH is built this way, the same structure lets us run this at the Simplicity level — not just NIW: any team, brand, or label can stand up its own CIH, with its own seats.

Bounded by design — the trust model
Deny by default Review before acting Tools scoped per seat Live client data gated (§10) Secrets never exposed Owner is the sole approver Everything auditable

Unlimited helpfulness, bounded authority. The portal always tries to help within a seat's job; it can never act outside that seat, change its own rules, or touch live client data until the owner signs off — out-of-scope asks are routed to a "Submit a request" queue the owner triages.
